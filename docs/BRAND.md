# ARTHA AI — Brand Guide

## Official name

**ARTHA AI**

## Positioning line

**Economic Intelligence. Opportunity. Wealth Creation.**

## Tagline

**Discover Value. Engineer Opportunity. Build Wealth.**

## Full form (brand acronym)

| Letter | Stands for | Meaning |
|--------|------------|--------|
| **A** | Advanced | Advanced AI reasoning, automation, and multi-agent intelligence |
| **R** | Research | Market research, economic signals, emerging trends, underserved opportunities |
| **T** | Technology | Scalable global AI SaaS connecting agents, tools, and data sources |
| **H** | Human-Centered | Human-approved decisions, transparent risks, practical strategies |
| **A** | Advancement | Validated opportunities → measurable progress, growth, and wealth creation |

## Brand promise

Make better-informed decisions about where opportunity exists and how to pursue it responsibly.

## Product one-liner

An AI-powered opportunity discovery and execution platform that combines market intelligence, multi-agent analysis, financial modelling, risk validation, and human-approved execution to help people identify and evaluate opportunities—from micro-capital experiments to scalable businesses.

## Voice & tone

- Professional, clear, evidence-based
- Optimistic but not hype-driven
- Transparent about risk and uncertainty
- Practical for entrepreneurs and small investors

## What to emphasize

1. Economic intelligence (research, signals, trends)
2. Opportunity discovery (gaps, resale, services)
3. Multi-agent analysis (researcher, analyst, planner, risk, strategy)
4. Micro-capital realism (costs, downside, targets)
5. Human approval before execution
6. Outcomes and learning, not just predictions

## Naming note

“Artha” is a Sanskrit-derived word associated with wealth, value, and purpose. The ARTHA expansion above is a **brand acronym**, not the word’s literal linguistic translation. Trademark and domain availability have not been verified in this document.

## For AI agents building this product

When generating UI copy, reports, or docs for this project:

- Use **ARTHA AI** as the product name (not OpportunityAI in user-facing text)
- Prefer the tagline and positioning line above
- Keep finance language precise; never invent numbers
- Stress human approval and risk transparency (the “H” in ARTHA)
